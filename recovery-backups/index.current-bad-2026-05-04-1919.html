What is RCS?

RCS messages bring brand identity, rich content and one-tap decisions.

RCS puts your business name, logo, imagery and reply actions inside the messaging app customers already use for texts. No separate download, no social app, and SMS can still carry the essential fallback message.

  • RCS messages give your business a branded sender profile, logo and verified sender identity.
  • Richer cards, media and carousel-style messages make offers easier to see and act on.
  • One-tap response buttons let your receiver make a clear decision without the need for a typed reply.
  • RightOnQ™ software keeps messages, replies, timing and follow-up attached to one card.
  • RCS approval commonly takes around four to six weeks, so it is worth getting ready early.

RCS messages are easy to trust and harder to ignore.

FAQ

Can messages include reply buttons?

Yes. RCS supports suggested replies and actions. Exact limits can vary by implementation, so campaign controls should be checked before launch.

Do you handle our RCS registration?

We can help, but it is not default. Registration involves a cycle of work and some customer input, so managed onboarding is charged separately.

Who do you work with?

RightOnQ currently works with UK-registered companies or businesses with a UK registered office. Recipients can be outside the UK where messaging rules allow.
