2. The Service

RightOnQ provides a B2B scheduled messaging and confirmation platform. The service enables business clients to upload their own recipient lists, schedule messages and capture confirmations and replies, with delivery via the following channels: SMS, RCS Business Messaging, and (where enabled) WhatsApp Business API.

Messaging is delivered using third-party messaging infrastructure providers. Depending on a Customer’s configuration, the underlying provider for that Customer will be either Twilio Inc. or Sent, Inc.; both are named as sub-processors in our Privacy Policy. Where RCS is unavailable to a recipient (for example, on an unsupported device or carrier), the platform will automatically fall back to SMS.

For each Customer, we typically provision a dedicated subaccount (or equivalent isolation) within the assigned messaging provider so that usage, billing, webhooks and logs are isolated to that Customer.
