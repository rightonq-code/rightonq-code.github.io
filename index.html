Good to know

RCS questions.

Do recipients need to install anything?

No. RCS arrives in the phone's native messaging app on supported devices. If RCS is not available, the essential message can fall back to SMS.

Is RightOnQ GDPR-compliant?

RightOnQ is built to support UK GDPR compliance. We’re a UK company, process data under UK GDPR, and only store the contact details you provide plus the message logs needed for delivery and audit. Your organisation remains responsible for recipient consent, lawful basis and opt-out handling.

Can messages include reply buttons?

Yes. Rich RCS cards can include up to four buttons, and carousel cards usually support one or two buttons per card. That means your audience can choose a clear next step with one tap, without typing a reply.

What happens when RCS is not available?

The message can fall back to SMS so the recipient still receives the essential information. Rich RCS elements such as images, carousels and buttons may not appear in the SMS version, and fallback SMS is charged at the applicable fallback message rate.

Do you handle RCS registration?

We can help, but it is not automatic. RCS registration needs business details, sender profile information, logo and banner assets, contact details, privacy and terms links, example messages, opt-in/opt-out information and approval checks. Some of that has to come from you. If you want us to support the process closely, managed onboarding is available from around £500.
